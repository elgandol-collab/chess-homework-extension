# Privacy Policy — Chess Homework Trainer

*Last updated: see the release date of the version you installed.*

**Summary: the extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.**

## What the extension stores (locally, on your device only)
- The homework files you open and your progress in them (moves played, time spent per move, number of wrong attempts).
- Your puzzle rating, language and sound preferences.

This data is kept with the browser's `chrome.storage.local`. It never leaves your device unless **you** choose to export it.

## What leaves your device
Nothing, automatically. The extension makes no network requests of its own: the chess engine (Stockfish, WebAssembly) and all assets are bundled inside the extension and run locally.

If you press **Export result**, a `.zip` file (PGN, text report, JSON) is saved to your computer, and you decide how to send it to your coach. The **WhatsApp** button only opens `wa.me/<coach number>` in a new tab; the coach's number comes from the homework file you opened, and nothing is attached or sent for you.

## Permissions
- `storage` — to remember your progress and settings on this device.
No host permissions, no access to the pages you browse, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising.

## Children
The extension has no accounts and collects no personal information, so it can be used by students of any age.
